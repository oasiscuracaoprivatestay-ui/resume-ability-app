## 2. In-Depth Reconciliation Findings

### A. The Seven Diet-Abilities
- **Phase 35 State:** Only Resume-Ability was codified. Appetite and Delay were marked provisional. Abilities 4 through 7 were uncodified and flagged as knowledge gaps.
- **Manuscript Evidence:** All seven books exist, each providing an exhaustive, dedicated methodology:
  1. Book 1: Resume-Ability (Consistency & Recovery)
  2. Book 2: Loss-Maintenance Ability (The Umbrella Ability)
  3. Book 3: Appetite-Fix Ability (Hunger Retraining)
  4. Book 4: Insulin-Aware Ability (Signal Understanding)
  5. Book 5: Keto-Switching Ability (Fuel Flexibility)
  6. Book 6: Circadian Eating Ability (Temporal Alignment)
  7. Book 7: Micro-Fasting Ability (Manageable Progressive Blocks)
- **Resolution:** All seven Diet-Abilities are now formally codified with canonical IDs in `CANONICAL_SEVEN_DIET_ABILITIES`. Inquiries about any of the seven abilities are now fully supported with Level 1 manuscript authority.

### B. "Delay Ability" vs "15-Minute Resume Method"
- **Phase 35 State:** "Delay Ability" was treated as a distinct ability due to the 15-minute urge timer.
- **Manuscript Evidence:** Sergio Laurant does not dedicate a separate book to "Delay Ability." Instead, the 15-minute pause is introduced in Book 1 Chapter 11 as "The 15-Minute Resume Method" and in Book 7 Chapter 11 as "The 15-Minute Block."
- **Resolution:** Reclassified as an app-specific behavioral technique (`APP_SPECIFIC`) belonging jointly to Resume-Ability and Micro-Fasting. The 15-minute Urge Timer remains an active, rewarded feature.

### C. Near-Slip vs. Slip vs. Resume
- **Phase 35 State:** Strict boundary separating Near-Slip, Completed Slip, and Resume.
- **Manuscript Evidence:**
  - Book 1 Chapter 14 emphasizes the "Seven Signals" where an urge is caught before action occurs.
  - Book 7 Chapter 15 defines "The Craving Block" as creating space for choice to return.
  - Book 1 Chapter 2 explicitly titles: "Resume-Ability Is Not Restarting." Resuming is picking up structure immediately, coexisting with the slip record.
- **Resolution:** Perfect alignment. Near-Slip remains an awareness milestone (+0 slip count). Resume remains an independent dimension.

### D. 20% OFF TRACK vs. 80/20 Principle
- **Phase 35 State:** 20% OFF TRACK is an On-Track outcome earning +5 points.
- **Manuscript Evidence:**
  - Book 1 Chapter 13: "The 80/20 Rule of Diet Consistency — Let Go of Perfect or You Will Never Get to Consistent."
  - Book 2 Chapter 4: "The 80/20 Principle: Why Perfection Fails."
- **Resolution:** Complete harmony. The app's `20% OFF TRACK` outcome directly operationalizes Sergio's 80/20 philosophy, protecting users from the all-or-nothing trap.

### E. App-Specific Behavioral Reinforcement
- **Phase 35 State:** Points are never subtracted; slips earn +8 points for honesty; recommitting earns +15 points; lifetime XP and levels are permanent.
- **Manuscript Evidence:** Book 1 Chapter 24 & Book 7 Chapter 47 emphasize that moralizing food, using shame-based punishment, or treating setbacks as character defects reliably destroys consistency.
- **Resolution:** Maintained as authoritative Level 2 application logic. Rewarding honest reporting creates the psychological safety required for behavioral change.
